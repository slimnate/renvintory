Renvintory UI Mockups

Ten mobile-first directions. Each file shows the same four screens stacked: home, location, report, and count. Open them on your phone to judge them at real size.

Modern directions

  • 01 · Focused Utility Light, calm, bottom tab bar. Safe and modern.
  • 02 · Dark Bar Mode High contrast, huge numerals. Built for a dim venue.
  • 03 · Neo-Brutalist Bold Thick borders, hard shadows, enormous tap targets.
  • 04 · iOS-Native Lists Grouped inset lists, segmented controls, bottom sheet.

Renaissance faire directions

  • 05 · Illuminated Ledger Parchment, rubricated drop caps, ruled ledger lines.
  • 06 · Tavern Signboard Painted boards, gold leaf, an emblem per location.
  • 07 · Heraldic Modern Shield badges and banners over a clean, fast base.

Signboard × Heraldic hybrids

Two ways of crossing 06 and 07: one keeps the dark materials, one keeps the light base.
